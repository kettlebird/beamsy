Mirror your iPhone to your Android TV. No ads. No subscription. Your first hour is in Full HD, free.

Open Beamsy on your TV, then on your iPhone open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring and pick your TV. There is nothing to install on the iPhone. Your screen and sound show up on the TV.

FREE
- Your first hour of mirroring is in Full HD (1080p) at 60 frames per second
- After that: 720p at 30 frames per second, with sound, 15 minutes at a time. Reconnect to keep going
- Music and other audio from your iPhone is never limited
- No ads, no account
- Upright videos and reels fill the sides with a soft blur instead of black bars
- Shows your TV's Wi-Fi signal, and warns you when a weak connection may make the picture stutter (you can turn the warning off)

UNLIMITED (OPTIONAL)
- Mirror without breaks, in Full HD (1080p) at 60 frames per second
- $6.99, paid once with an in-app purchase. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription

SETTINGS
- Rename your TV so it is easy to spot in the list
- Start Beamsy when the TV turns on, so your TV is always ready
- Open Beamsy automatically when an iPhone connects
- Ask for a 4-digit code before anyone can mirror
- Let a second iPhone take over, or keep the first one connected
- Shrink the picture for TVs that cut off the edges

GOOD TO KNOW
- One iPhone at a time
- iPhone and iPad only for now
- Your TV and iPhone must be on the same Wi-Fi or network
- Protected video (for example from some streaming apps) cannot be mirrored

Mirror your iPhone to your Android TV. No ads. No subscription. No time limit.

Open the app on your TV, then pick your TV from Screen Mirroring in your iPhone's Control Center. The app is AirPlay-compatible, so there is nothing to install on the iPhone. Your screen and sound show up on the TV.

FREE
- Mirrors at up to 720p, with sound
- No time limit, no ads, no account
- Music and other audio-only playback shows when audio is playing from your iPhone

FULL HD (OPTIONAL)
- Install the separate "Screen Mirroring TV Full HD Unlock" app for 1080p mirroring
- $6.99, paid once. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription

GOOD TO KNOW
- It works while the app is open on your TV
- One iPhone at a time
- iPhone only for now; Android phones are planned
- You can rename your TV so it is easy to spot in the list
- Your TV and iPhone must be on the same Wi-Fi or network
- Protected video (for example from some streaming apps) cannot be mirrored
